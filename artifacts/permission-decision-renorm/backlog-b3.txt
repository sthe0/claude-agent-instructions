agentctl order-dispose --as admits exactly two values, covered and cut. An order element that a plan covers PARTIALLY has no representation: it must be recorded as cut with the qualification buried in the free-text --reason. Observed 2026-08-11: a plan's headline hypothesis was tested on one self-collected case (n = 6) but could not be tested at population scale, and the scope-coverage block the user approves therefore presents it as "cut" followed by a paragraph of prose explaining that it is in fact partially covered. Prose inside a reason field is a workaround around a missing type, and the coverage block is precisely the artifact the user reads to decide what the plan does not do. Proposed shape: a third value (e.g. reduced) requiring two explicit fields — what is kept and what is dropped — so the reduction is machine-visible in the essence rather than legible only to a careful reader.
