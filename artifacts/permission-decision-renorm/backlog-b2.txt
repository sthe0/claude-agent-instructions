A plan-review finding carries only the BLOCKING / NON-BLOCKING distinction, and every BLOCKING verdict routes the same way: revise the plan and re-review. There is no verb for a finding whose content is "the study/task cannot be done as specified" — an objection to the ORDER rather than to the plan. Observed cost, 2026-08-11: round 2 of a twelve-round series reported that the headline hypothesis's response variable has zero valid observations on the existing record — i.e. the premise was invalid — and the loop processed it as an ordinary revise finding. Eight further rounds followed, costing $15.29 and roughly 62 minutes, and ran zero measurements, because each round could only ask the author to rewrite a plan whose defect was not in the plan. Proposed shape: a third finding class that routes to the order (escalate to the user for re-scoping) instead of to the plan author, and that the engine treats as terminal for the current plan rather than as another revise cycle.
