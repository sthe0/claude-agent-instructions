# A record that does not retain the variables a hypothesis relates leaves the hypothesis unanswerable

## Regularity

A hypothesis that relates two variables (for example review volume and finding count) cannot be confirmed or refuted on a record that did not retain those variables; it stays unanswerable however many rounds the record spans, and only instrumentation placed before the data is produced makes it testable.

## Generality

Level 1 — questions about a repeated process (a review loop, a retry cycle) asked of its own recorded history. It is not claimed for a hypothesis whose variables the record does retain.

## Induced from

- [[2026-08-11-review-loop-cannot-measure-its-own-convergence]] — a 12-round review loop kept round identity and verdict for 71 of 71 rounds but fix size for 15, finding count for 5 and finding text for none, so the volume hypothesis could not be tested on it.

## Refutation

Held-out check: the single source is the only context, so the claim is held at level 1 on it alone. A hypothesis relating two variables that was confirmed or refuted from a record retaining neither refutes it.
