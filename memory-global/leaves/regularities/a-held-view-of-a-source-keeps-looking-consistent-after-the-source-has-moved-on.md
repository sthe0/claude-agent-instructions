# a-held-view-of-a-source-keeps-looking-consistent-after-the-source-has-moved-on

## Regularity

A view of a source of truth that an agent holds locally (a checkout, a probe result, a remembered task status, the recorded condition of a standing constraint, a subordinate's report about itself, a backup of a mutable artifact) reflects the source only as of the moment it was read and stays internally consistent while the source changes, so integrity of the view says nothing about its currency; when such a view disagrees with a fact someone else states, the disagreement more often comes from the view being stale or incomplete than from the stated fact being false.

## Generality

Level 1 — ranges over any locally held copy, probe result, remembered status or report that stands in for a live source an agent could re-read. It is deliberately not claimed for sources that cannot be re-read, nor for facts stated by someone with no better access to the source than the holder of the view.

## Induced from

- [[doubt-own-snapshot]] — contributes the observed cases behind the statement: a stale main, a bare probe, a closure claim made from memory, a subordinate's self-report, an expired constraint and a backup compared with its own source.

## Refutation

A sample of apparent contradictions between a user's stated fact and the agent's locally held view in which, after refreshing from the authoritative source, most of the stated facts turn out to be false; or a held view that is shown to lag its source in no case after the source changed.
