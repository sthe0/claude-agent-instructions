# A remedy recorded in prose or memory is not in front of the author at the moment of the act it governs unless some trigger puts it there

## Regularity

A remedy that has been recorded — a memory leaf, a rule in a policy file, a lesson in an experience record — is not present at the moment of the act it governs unless something places it there: the author at that moment works from the brief, the code in front of them and what they retrieve, and a recorded remedy that no trigger surfaces is simply not among those. The same defect therefore recurs against a remedy that exists, was read once and was never placed in front of the next author.

## Generality

Level 2 — ranges over norms recorded for future acts of an agent or a person. It is not claimed for a norm embedded in the act's own mechanism (a gate that refuses the act), where presence is enforced.

## Refutation

A recorded remedy with no trigger into its act that, over many later occurrences of that act, was applied without recurrence of the defect would show that for that remedy recording alone made it present.
