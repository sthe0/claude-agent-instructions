# a-ground-that-is-not-recorded-experience-names-no-refuting-observation

## Regularity

A choice in a plan rests on something; when that something is a recorded generalization of experience — a regularity, or a single experience as its trivial case — it names the observations that would refute it, and the choice can be checked against them. When it is a rule, a prohibition, a path or a quote, it names no such observation: the choice cannot be refuted through its ground, and a wrong ground stays invisible until the choice fails. An order quote that no existing regularity covers is itself an observation of the customer's situation, from which a new regularity can be generalized and then relied on.

## Generality

Level 1 — ranges over plans in this agent system whose stages name a ground for a prescription they adopt. It is deliberately not claimed for choices made without any stated ground, nor for the correctness of a ground's content, only for whether the ground names what would refute it.

## Refutation

Refuted by a plan whose choice rests on a rule, a prohibition, a path or a quote alone, whose ground is later found wrong through a refuting observation that the ground itself named; or by a rule-form ground that carries a named observation which would refute it. Also forced broader if a ground of some further kind turns out to name refuting observations as a regularity does.
