# a-success-condition-edited-by-the-party-it-judges-gets-no-independent-read

## Regularity

A criterion that an executor edits in flight, even when it can see the criterion is wrong and the correction is in the plan's favour, changes the thing the work is measured against without any actor other than the one being measured reading the change. The plan then stops being the thing the work was judged against, and obviousness of the correction is what makes the edit tempting; the gated path (declare, investigate, critique, normalize, replan, with a fresh review bound to the moved plan) costs more and buys exactly the independent read the in-flight edit lacks.

## Generality

Level 1 — ranges over control criteria of plan stages that the executing party can edit while the stage is running. It is deliberately not claimed for criteria changed through the gated replan path, which carries an independent review.

## Induced from

- [[plan-control-criterion-hygiene]] — contributes the fourth norm and its rationale: an executor who notices a broken criterion and fixes it in flight leaves the plan no longer the thing the work was measured against.

## Refutation

In-flight edits of a criterion by its executor found, in a sample, to be corrected by a later independent review no more often than gated replans of criteria are.
