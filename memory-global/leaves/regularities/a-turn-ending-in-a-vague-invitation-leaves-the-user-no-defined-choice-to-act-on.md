# a-turn-ending-in-a-vague-invitation-leaves-the-user-no-defined-choice-to-act-on

## Regularity

A turn that ends with a vague invitation ('let me know if...', 'just say the word', 'you decide') has the shape of a handoff but names no choice and no recommended option, so the user has nothing to click and must reopen the conversation to learn what the decision was; across six sessions users had to ask what to say or what comes next after turns ending this way.

## Generality

Level 1 — ranges over agent turns that end at a decision point in a conversation with a user. It is deliberately not claimed for turns that end by stating a completed result with nothing left to decide.

## Induced from

- [[hedge-phrase-not-a-question]] — contributes the transcript findings across six sessions, where users had to ask what to say or what comes next after a turn ending in a hedge phrase.

## Refutation

Turns ending with a hedge phrase after which users answer or proceed without having to ask what the choice was or what comes next.
