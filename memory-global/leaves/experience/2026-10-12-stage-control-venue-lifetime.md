# A stage control addresses a venue the delivery's own later steps delete, restore or never populated

## Difficulty
A stage check is authored against the venue that exists mid-execution: a delivery worktree, a repo_root pinned to it, a scaffold, a settings file. The delivery's own later steps (landing, cleanup, a stage that restores or deletes the tree) then remove or replace that venue, so verify-final re-runs the check against a place that no longer holds the artifacts, or that holds only the pre-change baseline and passes for the wrong reason. The venue's lifetime is a property the author never stated, and the engine could not infer it from a `cd <path>` in the command text.

**Functional ground:** A control's declared venue (the checkout, worktree or file it observes) is treated as a fixed place, but the engine re-runs stage checks at verify-final after the delivery's own landing and cleanup have destroyed, restored or replaced that place.
