# Case: evocative-sample-label-without-provenance

## Aspect

A name or adjective attached to a dataset asserts a property, such as liveness or canonical status, that the evidence in the report does not cover.

## Expected

Each named dataset carries its provenance, and every adjective is earned by something the report states.

## Actual

Two samples were published with labels implying properties never established, and a first correction cited the wrong source.

## Mismatch

The label claimed more than the evidence domain supported, and the correction was also unchecked against its source.
